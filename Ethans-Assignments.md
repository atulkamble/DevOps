## 📝 **Assignment 1: Evolution of DevOps (Theory + Analysis)**

**Objective:**
Understand the evolution from Waterfall → Agile → DevOps.

**Tasks:**

1. Explain **Waterfall model phases** with one real-world example
2. List **limitations of traditional SDLC**
3. Compare **Agile vs Waterfall** (minimum 5 points)
4. Explain **challenges faced by Ops teams in Agile**
5. Write a short note on **why DevOps was needed**

**Submission:**

* PDF / Google Doc
* Minimum 2 pages
